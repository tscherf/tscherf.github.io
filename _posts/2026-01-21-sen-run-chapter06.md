The day begins with a leisurely breakfast at the Serai Restaurant. Whenever I
ask about the Executive Lounge, the answers are somewhat evasive. Yesterday’s
Happy Hour also took place in the atrium, which makes me suspect that there
currently isn’t a lounge at all. Interestingly, the Diamond welcome letter
explicitly states that Happy Hour is only held in the atrium if the lounge is
unavailable. A bit odd.

A stop at Hashem Restaurant—famous for its falafel and hummus—in the middle of
the bustling downtown area is, of course, a must.

On the way to the Roman Theatre, I also stop by the King Hussein Mosque.

![Downtown 7](/assets/images/2026-01/21/IMG_1389.jpeg)

Up close, the Roman Theatre feels even larger than it already looked from the
Citadel hill.

![Downtown 8](/assets/images/2026-01/21/IMG_1401.jpeg)

On the way back to the hotel, I make one more stop at the King Abdullah I
Mosque, better known as the Blue Mosque. A prayer is taking place inside at the
time, so I limit my visit to the exterior. I’m sure I’ll be back another time.
