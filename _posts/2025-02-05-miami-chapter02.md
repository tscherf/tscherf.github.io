Chapter 02

After many hours in the SEN Lounge, boarding for LX1025 finally began at 6:00 PM
at Gate A25. It was clear from the start that this flight would be completely
full—Business Class was fully booked, and Economy didn’t look any better.

![LX1025 boarding](/assets/images/2025-02/05/lx-1025.png)

Since I had already eaten enough in the lounge, I only had water during the
flight. Despite the full load, the service was excellent. The bread basket came
around multiple times, and drinks were refilled twice automatically. Of course,
I couldn’t skip the classic Swiss chocolate. 🍫

![Swiss chocolate](/assets/images/2025-02/05/swiss-chocolate.png)

We landed ahead of schedule at 7:30 PM in Zurich. Since my luggage was only
checked through to ZRH, I had to wait at the baggage carousel for about 10
minutes. Luckily, my suitcase was the second one out, so by 7:45 PM, I was
already at the hotel shuttle stand, waiting for my transfer to the Hilton
Airport Hotel.

![Hotel shuttle](/assets/images/2025-02/05/shuttle.png)

The shuttle runs every 20 minutes, on the hour, at -1° Celsius (not ideal, but
manageable). The transfer costs 5 CHF each way, payable either directly to the
driver or digitally via QR code at the bus stop or schedule display. A very
efficient system!

About 10 minutes later, I arrived at the hotel. Unfortunately, the executive
lounge closed at 8:00 PM, so I was given two vouchers for the hotel bar instead.

I received a complimentary upgrade to an Executive Room with a Queen Bed—a King
Room wasn’t available at the time of booking. The room itself was okay but
nothing special. My welcome gift included a standard card (without a personal
note) and two bottles of water—acceptable, but not impressive.

![Hilton ZRH Airport hotel](/assets/images/2025-02/05/hilton.png)
![Hilton ZRH Airport hotel](/assets/images/2025-02/05/room1.png)
![Hilton ZRH Airport hotel](/assets/images/2025-02/05/room2.png)
![Hilton ZRH Airport hotel](/assets/images/2025-02/05/room3.png)
![Hilton ZRH Airport hotel](/assets/images/2025-02/05/room4.png)

Since I had to book this room last-minute earlier in the day, I used 40,000
Hilton Honors points for the stay. Let’s see if I can somehow get those points
refunded. 😊

At the hotel bar, I exchanged my two vouchers for two Cokes and then went
straight to my room. Despite spending most of the day just sitting in the
lounge, I was still exhausted and looking forward to a comfortable bed.

![Hotel bar](/assets/images/2025-02/05/bar2.png)
![Hotel bar](/assets/images/2025-02/05/bar3.png)
![Hotel bar](/assets/images/2025-02/05/bar4.png)

Good news — my tooth held up well, and I had no pain throughout the journey.
Fingers crossed 🤞 that it stays that way for the long-haul flight.

Fun Fact: My Original Flight Was Delayed! Ironically, LX064 from ZRH to MIA had
a 90-minute delay today. Maybe I could have made it after all.

Now, I’ll be flying LX064 on HB-JNB, which is one year newer than HB-JNA and has
only eight years of service. 😊
